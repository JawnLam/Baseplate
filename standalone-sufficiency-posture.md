# Baseplate — Standalone Sufficiency Posture

> Why use Baseplate instead of a general LLM? The answer rests on the substrate (the 47-requirement standalone-sufficiency framework), not a sales pitch. Source of truth: `_meta/posture.yaml`. Scorecard: `_meta/vetting-rubric-filled.md`.

## The two master tests (answered)

- **Displacement** (*can a ten-minute prompt reproduce this?*): a prompt can produce *a* PRD, and a good one a decent stack. It cannot enforce a selection protocol with recorded exclusion rationale, maintain numbered-requirement + traceability discipline as a gate, administer a stranger test as a ship block, or carry a portfolio failure catalog so product #4 benefits from #1-3. The durable core is the **gates + the accumulating catalog**, not the templates.
- **Absorption** (*will platforms ship this in ~12 months?*): partially — generic "spec/plan modes" are becoming table stakes, so template-quality generation is commodity. What platforms will not ship: the operator's selection doctrine, the portfolio-level lesson log, per-stack traceability enforced as a gate, and the stranger test as a ship ritual. Investment is weighted toward gates, state, and the failure catalog; templates are treated as commodity scaffolding.

## Domain stakes

`low` — failure is bounded to the operator's own time/effort (a defective stack is caught by the gates or revised). The 8 TG conditional gates are `n-a`.

## T0 hard gates (all met)

| Gate | Disposition | Evidence |
|------|-------------|----------|
| REQ-A1 Capability Parity | met | Runs on any capable model; leans on general knowledge + the baked-in canon |
| REQ-A2 Graceful Scope Boundaries | met | AUDIT-STACK on any-origin stacks; no benign-request refusal |
| REQ-A3 No Artificial Lobotomy | met | Answers adjacent questions; discipline lives at the ship gate |
| REQ-B1 Persistent User Model | met | Per-cartridge state + cross-cartridge portfolio catalog |
| REQ-H4 Time-to-First-Value | met | Session one produces a locked selection + the unconditional L1+L4 docs |

## Moat commitments

- **REQ-M1 Data Flywheel** — `_portfolio/failure-catalog.md`: every cartridge close-out contributes failure modes, compounding across the portfolio. A general LLM pointed at the same task has no such accumulated catalog.
- **REQ-M2 Legitimate Switching Cost** — the accumulated per-product stacks + portfolio catalog become the operator's system of record; leaving means leaving real value.

## Verdict

See `_meta/vetting-rubric-filled.md`: **Defensible specialist** — the moat rests on accumulated proprietary portfolio state and enforced gates, not on promptable behavior.
